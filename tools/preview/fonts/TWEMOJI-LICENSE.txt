TwemojiMozilla.ttf — Twemoji Mozilla 0.7.0 (https://github.com/mozilla/twemoji-colr), the COLRv0
build of Twitter's Twemoji that Roblox also ships as content/fonts/TwemojiMozilla.ttf.

Used only by the GUI preview renderer (tools/preview/gui/twemoji.py) so the previews draw emoji
the way the game does. It is not part of the game.
